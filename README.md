## Research Impact Statement 🌍  
My research applies machine learning to medicine, first in psychiatry and now in primary care. In my PhD I built models that estimate overdose risk from provincial health records, and I developed clinical decision support systems around them. At IKIM I work on federated learning so that clinical models can be trained across practices without moving patient data. The aim throughout is earlier and more reliable risk identification (e.g. overdose, mental health comorbidities, and suicide risk), so that interventions can happen before crises do.
